Find the source of the outbreak before the clock runs out.

A free, self-paced epidemiology course written like an approachable textbook, with quizzes, an assignment, and a role play. Learn to read an epi curve, calculate attack rates, and work a case from first report to control measures.

Start the course

Seven units, 23 lessons. Go at your own pace, start any time.

What you will learn

  1. Unit 1Introduction to the course

    How the course works and how to get the most from it.

  2. Unit 2Introduction to epidemiology

    What epidemiology is, clinical vs. public health, key terminology, the natural history of disease, and infectious vs. noninfectious diseases. Ends with a quiz.

  3. Unit 3Epidemiological history

    How the field began, the epidemiologists who shaped it, and the health events that changed it. Ends with a quiz.

  4. Unit 4Disease transmission and prevention

    The agent-host-environment triad, the chain of infection, modes of transmission, and prevention for different agents. Ends with an assignment.

  5. Unit 5Public health surveillance

    The basics, the 5 steps of surveillance, and surveillance technologies. Ends with a role play: seek an unknown pathogen.

  6. Unit 6Epidemiological data and visualization

    Epi curves, subdivided tables, line listings, and cluster maps. Ends with a quiz.

  7. Unit 7Epidemiological calculations

    Measures of disease frequency, measures of effect, the “rates” formula, and diagnostic accuracy measures. Ends with a final test.

Try one first

120 people attend a school picnic. 48 later develop vomiting and diarrhea. What is the attack rate?

Show the answer

Attack rate = ill ÷ exposed = 48 ÷ 120 = 0.40, or 40%. In the course, you will learn to compare attack rates for each food served to find the likely source.

Questions

Who is this course for?

Middle school, high school, and postsecondary students preparing for HOSA Disease Detectives, and anyone curious about how outbreaks are solved.

How does the course work?

You read each unit’s lessons, then check what stuck with a quiz, an assignment, or a role play.

Do I need a science background?

No. If you can do basic fractions and percentages, you can follow along.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no payments and no card required.

Is this official HOSA material?

No. It is an independent study course. Always check the current HOSA competitive events guidelines for your event.
